Job Description

As a Technical Business Analyst / Technical Product Manager at Postman, you will be responsible for supporting the development, enhancement, and maintenance of the platform's suite of applications. You will work at the intersection of business, technology, and user experience — thriving in a fast-paced environment, enjoying problem-solving, and eager to grow within product management.

Your role will involve partnering with Product Managers, Engineering, QA, and business stakeholders to translate business needs into actionable requirements, ensure quality, and contribute to the overall product strategy.

Key Responsibilities

  • Gather, analyze, and document business system requirements — ensuring all user stories and acceptance criteria are clear, complete, and testable to both product and engineering.
  • Act as a liaison between business and technical teams by gathering feedback, managing expectations, and clearly communicating updates, risks, and changes to stakeholders.
  • Support quality assurance and user acceptance testing by helping define test scenarios, validating outcomes against requirements, and ensuring defects are properly tracked and resolved.
  • Track product performance metrics by retrieving data, creating reports, and analyzing the output to ensure meeting or exceeding targets established for the project.
  • Maintain and update comprehensive product documentation — requirements, process flows, system interactions, and release notes — ensuring information is accurate, accessible, and aligns with the current system functionality and business processes.

Qualifications Required

  • Bachelor's degree in business, information systems, or related field.
  • 2–5 years of experience in Product Management, Business Analysis, or related roles.
  • Experience working in Agile environments (Scrum or Kanban).
  • Strong technical skills with the ability to effectively communicate ideas and collaborate with, as well as motivate, the development team.
  • Comfort operating in ambiguity and balancing competing priorities.
  • Fluent English speaker.

You will work with the teams behind an API platform that millions of developers use every day — bringing your best ideas forward, collaborating across Product, Engineering and Design, and making a real impact. The role comes with mentorship and learning programs to help you grow.

The team is looking for people excited to work with advanced technologies, provide best-in-class developer experiences, drive the development and deployment of scalable solutions, and guide agile teams through meaningful change.

If you are self-motivated, collaborative, innovative, and have a passion for developer tools, this role as a Technical Business Analyst / Technical Product Manager at Postman might be your next big career move. Don't wait — apply today.

What success looks like in 90 days

  • You have shadowed key stakeholders across Product, Engineering, and QA, and built relationships with the principal customers of the platform.
  • You can articulate the current backlog, ongoing initiatives, and the biggest customer-impacting issues against the roadmap.
  • You are independently writing crisp PRDs and acceptance criteria for at least one workstream.
  • You're contributing to release planning rituals and helping the team unblock decisions quickly.
